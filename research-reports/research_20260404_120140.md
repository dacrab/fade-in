# ebarber.gr vs. Your App — Feature Gap Analysis
> Generated: 2026-04-04 | Based on live ebarber.gr research + full codebase exploration

---

## Executive Summary

ebarber.gr is a mature Greek barbershop booking SaaS (running since 2022, 350+ shops, 150k+ customers) built around three pillars: **real-time booking**, **business management tooling**, and **community/network effects**. Your app has a solid end-to-end booking core (discovery → booking → review → management + admin), but is missing **entire product surface areas** that ebarber monetizes and uses for retention. The gaps are not minor polish items — several are strategic differentiators (payments, push notifications, mobile apps, job board, barber community) that define ebarber's moat.

## Detailed Gap Breakdown by Theme

---

### 1. 📲 Notifications & Reminders
**ebarber has**: Automatic push notifications on iOS & Android, free SMS reminders, Viber reminders, and email reminders — all sent automatically on booking confirmation and as appointment approaches.

**Your app has**: Nothing. No notification system exists in the codebase (no email sending, no SMS/Viber integration, no push notification setup).

- No email transactional sending (e.g. Resend, SendGrid)
- No SMS/Viber gateway (e.g. Twilio, Vonage)
- No web push notifications (no service worker, no manifest)
- **Impact**: High churn risk — customers forget appointments, no-show rates spike, owners lose revenue

---

### 2. 💳 Payments & No-Show Protection
**ebarber has**: Full online payment processing with cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Payments act as no-show protection — shops collect fees if customers cancel late or don't show.

**Your app has**: Price display only (formatPrice utility, price stored in cents). Zero payment infrastructure.

- No payment processor integration (Stripe, Viva Wallet, etc.)
- No deposit/prepayment flow in booking modal
- No cancellation policy or refund logic
- **Impact**: This is a major monetization and trust feature. Without it, the platform can't protect shop owners from no-shows.

---

### 3. 📱 Mobile Apps & PWA
**ebarber has**: Native iOS app (App Store, 4.9★, rated), native Android app (Play Store), PWA with service worker, "Add to Homescreen" support.

**Your app has**: A web app only. No `manifest.json`, no service worker, no mobile app.

- No Progressive Web App setup
- No native mobile wrappers (React Native, Capacitor, etc.)
- **Impact**: ebarber reaches customers where they spend time (phone home screens, app stores). Discovery via app stores is a growth channel your app misses entirely.

---

### 4. 🔐 Social Authentication
**ebarber has**: Google, Facebook, and Apple login in addition to email/password.

**Your app has**: Email/password only (Convex Auth with password provider).

- No OAuth providers configured (Google, Facebook, Apple)
- Registration friction is higher without social login
- **Impact**: Conversion drop at registration — social login significantly reduces signup abandonment.

---

### 5. 📊 Business Analytics & Reporting
**ebarber has**: Revenue tracking per day/period, appointment statistics, performance metrics — all accessible from mobile in "2 clicks."

**Your app has**: Admin-level stats (total shops, users, verified count). The owner dashboard (`/business`) has no revenue summary, no appointment trend charts, no earnings reports.

- No revenue aggregation queries in `convex/appointments.ts`
- No charts or time-series data for owners
- Admin stats are platform-level only, not per-shop for owners
- **Impact**: Owners can't see how their business is performing, reducing the app's value as a business tool.

---

### 6. 👤 Digital CRM / Customer Database
**ebarber has**: "Ψηφιακό Πελατολόγιο" — a digital customer registry that stores customer info per shop, supports GDPR-style compliance, and lets shops send/receive/update customer records.

**Your app has**: Customer data exists in the appointments table but there's no dedicated CRM view — owners cannot browse their customer list, see visit history per customer, add notes, or export data.

- No per-shop customer list query/view
- No customer visit history from owner's perspective
- No customer notes or tags
- **Impact**: Shops can't build relationships with returning customers — a core retention tool missing.

---

### 7. 🎫 Subscription / Monetization Model
**ebarber has**: Freemium + 3 paid tiers (Solo €25/mo, Duo €45/mo, Team €65/mo) with annual discount (20% off + 1 month free). Features are gated by plan (number of barbers, advanced features).

**Your app has**: No monetization layer at all. All features are equally accessible to all owners with no billing, no plans, no feature gating.

- No subscription/billing system
- No plan limits (e.g. barber count per tier)
- No payment collection from businesses
- **Impact**: The app currently has no revenue model.

---

### 8. 💼 Job Board
**ebarber has**: A dedicated "Αγγελίες Εργασίας" (Job Listings) section where barbershops post job openings and barbers can find work — a unique network-effect feature.

**Your app has**: Nothing remotely similar.

- No job posting model in schema
- No job listing routes or UI
- **Impact**: ebarber retains barbers as users (not just shops/customers), creating a 3-sided marketplace. Your app is 2-sided only.

---

### 9. 🤝 Barber Community
**ebarber has**: "Barber Community" — described as the first online community for barbers in Greece. Includes discussion forums, equipment buy/sell marketplace, industry event/seminar listings, and educational content.

**Your app has**: Nothing. No community features exist.

- No community/forum data model
- No marketplace for equipment
- No events/seminars board
- No blog
- **Impact**: ebarber has network-effect stickiness through community. Users stay on the platform even when not booking.

---

### 10. 📣 Marketing & Shop Promotion Tools
**ebarber has**: Targeted ads on Google & social media, a customizable booking link for social media sharing, branded QR code stickers for physical shops, and a social media management service offered to shops.

**Your app has**: None of these promotional tools exist.

- No shareable/embeddable booking link generation
- No QR code generation for shop booking pages
- No ad/promotion management
- **Impact**: Shops on ebarber get a marketing partner, not just software. This is a key differentiator for acquiring and retaining business customers.

## What Your App Does Better (or Differently)

- **Open source / self-hostable**: ebarber is a closed SaaS. If your app is open, shops can own their data.
- **Modern stack**: Convex + TanStack Router + Vite is more modern than what ebarber appears to run on.
- **Map-first discovery**: Your Leaflet map integration is on the listing page — ebarber's map visibility is less prominent.
- **Admin panel**: Your admin panel (verify shops, manage users) is more structured than what ebarber exposes publicly.
- **Review reply system**: Owner replies to reviews are implemented — it's unclear if ebarber has this.
